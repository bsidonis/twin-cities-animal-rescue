Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community-focused nonprofit dedicated to helping animals in need. We believe every animal deserves kindness, proper care, and the chance to become part of a loving family.

Through adoption, fostering, volunteering, and community involvement, our goal is to make life better for animals while connecting caring people with meaningful opportunities to help.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We treat animals and people with kindness and respect.

Trust

We aim to communicate honestly and responsibly with our community.

Teamwork

We believe lasting change happens when people work together.

Responsible Care

We encourage safe homes, proper animal care, and responsible adoption.
